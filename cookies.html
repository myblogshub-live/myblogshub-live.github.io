Cookies Policy

Last updated: January 2025

H.x.H uses minimal cookies to provide a smooth and personalized reading experience. This policy explains what cookies we use and why.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help websites remember your preferences and improve functionality.

Cookies We Use

  • Theme Preference — Remembers your dark/light mode choice
  • Session Cookie — Maintains your login session while you browse
  • Local Storage — Stores your newsletter subscription status and saved preferences locally

No Tracking Cookies

We do not use third-party tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics cookies that share data with external services. Your reading habits remain private.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect certain functionality like theme persistence or login sessions.

Updates

We may update this policy as needed. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
