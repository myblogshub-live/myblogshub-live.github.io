Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

At H.x.H, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our journal.

Information We Collect

When you subscribe to our newsletter or create an account, we collect your name and email address. We also collect anonymous usage data through standard web analytics to improve your reading experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To send you our weekly newsletter and journal updates
  • To personalize your reading experience
  • To improve our content and website functionality
  • To respond to your inquiries and feedback

Data Protection

Your data is stored securely and never shared with third parties for marketing purposes. We use industry-standard encryption and security practices to protect your information.

Cookies

We use minimal cookies for essential functionality — theme preferences and session management. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. See our Cookies Policy for details.

Your Rights

You may unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time. You can also request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, reach out to us at privacy@hxh.journal.
